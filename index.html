05 / Helpful information

Before you
drive.

How much does it cost to rent a car?

Rates depend on vehicle, dates and rental terms. Request a current quotation; older directory prices are not reliable current rates.

Can I get a car at Harare Airport?

Airport shuttle services are advertised. Ask about pickup arrangements and current availability.

What documents do I need?

Ask the company about age, licence, identity documents, deposit and insurance requirements before booking.

Can I travel outside Harare?

Confirm permitted routes, mileage limits, road conditions and any cross-border restrictions directly with the rental company.

Is the vehicle in the photo available?

Photos are category illustrations, not verified fleet inventory. Exact make, model and availability must be confirmed.
